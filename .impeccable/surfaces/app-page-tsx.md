# Home (and the storefront it opens)

Scope: `/` first, then shop, product, about, free resources, contact, FAQ, cart, order.
Visitor mode: Persuade (home, shop, product, free resources); Read (FAQ, about); Operate (cart, order, contact).
Audience and job: a parent, caregiver, preschool teacher or homeschool family, usually on a phone, finding
something affordable to do outdoors with a child of 3–6. Action: explore the shop, reach the Explorer Pack.
Content: the owner's brief only. No reviews, credentials, policies, addresses, dates or invented products.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site is one painted cut-paper meadow seen from a child's height. Refuses the airy
cream boutique: centred headline, three rounded cards, botanical corner sprigs.

OWN-WORLD: Sheets of hand-painted paper, cut with scissors and layered with soft contact shadows: cream
morning sky, sage hills, dusty-rose bloom, tan path, brown soil. Brush-streaked pigment in every sheet.
Anything not released yet is a pencil outline on bare paper, waiting for its piece to be glued on; what
is real is painted paper. Controls are cut tabs. One light from upper left.

STORY: This takes my small child outside to find real things; here is the first pack, what is in it,
what it costs, and honestly whether I can have it yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand name as large cut-paper lettering in two staggered lines in a cream sky, slogan and one
plain sentence beneath, "Explore the shop" beside it. Child-height flowers rise from the bottom edge past the
slogan on both sides; on wide screens a butterfly is perched on the letters. Along the front hill runs the hunt: five pencil outlines.
Signature interaction: tap the bee, butterfly, mushroom, acorn and rabbit hidden in the meadow and each
outline is glued in with its painted piece: the product's first page, working. Motion grammar: pieces lift,
turn a few degrees, settle; nothing floats or loops except one bee.

FORM: Painted cut-paper collage (picture-book and preschool process-art tradition), position 7 of 7.
Scroll descends in horizon bands: sky, hill, meadow, rose field, forest floor. seed:ebebccb8e540daf6

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Raises kept from the declined hand
- From the segment display: the unlit state is designed (pencil outline for unreleased).
- From the cyclorama: horizon bands order the page from sky to soil.
- From the ink basin: pigment varies inside every sheet; no flat vector fills at scale.
- From the gravity garden: one gravity; everything grows up from its band's lower edge.
