## Platform

## Stack
React / Next.js (settled at repo setup, see `docs/decisions.md`). Hosting, checkout, email and

## Users
Parents, caregivers, preschool teachers and homeschool families with children ages 3–6.
Mostly on a phone, between other things, looking for something affordable to do with a young
child today *(inferred)*. The owner is a second user: a beginner at websites who must be able to
change text, photos, colors and products without a developer.

## Product Purpose
Wildflower & Wonder creates nature-inspired educational activities that encourage young children
to explore the outdoors, develop creativity, learn through play, and become curious about the world
around them. Learning should be fun, hands-on, imaginative and connected to nature.
Business goal (internal, never shown to visitors): $500–$1,000 a month of additional income,
starting small with digital downloads (no inventory) and growing into kits, seasonal boxes, bundles.

## Positioning
Printable and hands-on activities that send a 3–6 year old outside to look at something real,
rather than keeping them at a table *(inferred from "get outside, explore, create, and learn together")*.

## Operating Context
A grown-up buys and prints a PDF at home or at a preschool, then takes the pages outdoors with
the child. Physical kits later ship to the buyer. The business is in Boise, Idaho, and is a sole
proprietorship.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sells digital downloads now; physical craft kits later. New products must be addable without
  rebuilding the site.
- Shop categories: Digital Downloads, Nature Crafts, Seasonal Activities, Activity Bundles,
  Physical Craft Kits.
- Pages: Home, Shop, product pages, About Us, Free Resources, Contact, FAQ, cart.
- First product: **My Little Nature Explorer Pack**, suggested price $6.99, nine-page printable PDF:
  nature scavenger hunt; leaf identification activity; flower coloring page; bug discovery worksheet;
  nature matching game; outdoor observation sheet; nature counting activity; create-your-own nature
  collage; Little Explorer certificate.
- **Never present a product as purchasable** until its finished PDF is uploaded and checkout and
  delivery really work. The same goes for the newsletter, contact form and free downloads.
- Undecided (owner): returns and refunds policy, shipping regions/costs/times, licence terms for
  classroom use, business email, domain name, kit contents and prices, release dates.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Wildflower & Wonder. Slogan: "Growing curious minds, one adventure at a time!"
- Feeling line: "Little hands, big discoveries, and a whole world of wonder."
- Colors: sage green, warm cream, dusty rose, soft earthy brown, natural tan.
- Motifs: wildflowers, butterflies, leaves, mushrooms, bees, acorns, woodland animals, nature
  illustrations, pressed-flower-inspired details.
- Must feel: warm and welcoming, whimsical but professional, playful and child-friendly, peaceful,
  easy for parents to navigate. Beautiful typography, soft colors, rounded elements, subtle
  illustrations, plenty of white space.
- Must not look: overly childish, cluttered, or like a generic school website.
- Voice: warm, personal, plain.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles
1. Every page points a family outdoors.
2. Say only what is true today; mark what is coming as coming.
3. The grown-up finds the thing, the price and the age range in seconds.
4. Adding product number two is copying one block, not a rebuild.
5. Small and real beats big and pretend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Easy-to-read text, mobile-first use, accessible and fast (WCAG 2.2 AA as the working bar *(inferred)*).
